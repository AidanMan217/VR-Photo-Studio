Scenes you export from the desktop app show up here automatically.

  1. On your PC, open VR Photo Studio and add a photo (or video).
  2. Pick an output such as 3D Scene (look behind), Object / person, VR180 or 3D photo, and keep “Add to VR viewer” ticked.
  3. Press Start. When it finishes, the scene appears on this page within a few seconds.
  4. Tap a scene, then press Enter VR (or Enter AR to see it in your room).
How do I open this on my Quest?
  1. Put the Quest on the same Wi-Fi network as your PC.
  2. In the desktop app, open the VR Viewer page and press Start viewer.
  3. On the Quest, open the Browser and type the https:// address shown in the app (or scan the QR code with the Quest camera).
  4. The first time, the browser warns that the connection is not private. That is expected for a home server: choose Advanced → Proceed.
  5. No Wi-Fi? Connect the Quest with a USB cable, choose USB mode in the app and open http://localhost:8080 on the Quest.
