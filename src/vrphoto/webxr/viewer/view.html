On this screen

  • Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • WASD move, Q/E down/up.
  • Space plays / pauses videos.

In VR (Quest controllers)

  • Left stick: walk. Right stick: turn in steps.
  • Right trigger: hold, aim at the floor, release to jump there (where available).
  • Grip: grab objects - use both grips to resize. Stick left/right rotates.
  • Y or B: menu (recenter, next scene, passthrough, exit).
  • You can also just walk and lean - your real movement is used.

With hand tracking

  • Pinch to click. Turn your left palm towards you and tap the blue dot for the menu.
